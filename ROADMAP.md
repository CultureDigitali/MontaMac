# MontaMac Roadmap

This roadmap communicates direction, not guaranteed dates. Priorities may change after testing and community feedback.

## Now

- Validate MontaMac 2.2 across FTP, SFTP and WebDAV servers
- Complete accessibility and keyboard-navigation testing
- Collect reliability feedback for automatic reconnection and cache limits

## Next

- Stronger installer recovery and dependency guidance
- Export and import of sanitized profile settings without credentials
- Menu bar controls and launch-at-login behavior

## Exploring

- Additional rclone-backed remote protocols
- Additional cache policies for low-disk-space environments
- More detailed transfer and storage insights

Have a concrete use case? Open a [feature request](https://github.com/CultureDigitali/MontaMac/issues/new/choose) and explain the problem you want to solve.
