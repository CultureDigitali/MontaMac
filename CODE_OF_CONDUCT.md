# MontaMac Community Code of Conduct

MontaMac welcomes users and contributors of every background and experience level.

## Our standards

We expect respectful, constructive and technically focused participation. Good contributions include patient questions, actionable feedback, inclusive language, clear attribution and respect for privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, deliberate disruption and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.

## Privacy and security

Do not post credentials, access tokens, private server addresses, personal mount paths or unredacted production logs. Security vulnerabilities must be reported privately according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [info@culturedigitali.eu](mailto:info@culturedigitali.eu). Culture Digitali Srl will review reports confidentially and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation or permanently exclude repeated or severe abuse.

Enforcement decisions will consider context, impact and proportionality. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern in good faith is prohibited.

This code applies to repository spaces and public interactions where someone represents the MontaMac project.
